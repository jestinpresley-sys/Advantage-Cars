What owners ask

What does Advantage take?

A flat share of each completed booking, agreed before your car is listed and unchanged for the length of the agreement. There is no listing fee, no monthly fee, and nothing owed for a month with no bookings.

Who covers damage?

Every renter is screened and carries protection on the booking. Damage claims are filed and pursued by us, and you're kept on the thread throughout. Your own policy is never the first line.

What kind of cars qualify?

Generally under eight years old, under 100,000 miles, clean title, and in good cosmetic condition. Economy and mid-size sedans move fastest. Send it through even if you're unsure — we'll tell you honestly.

Can I take the car back?

Yes, with thirty days' notice, or immediately once any confirmed booking on it has ended. There is no lock-in period.

Do I need to be in Memphis?

The car does. You don't — several owners live out of state and haven't seen their vehicle in a year.

About

Cheap shouldn't feel cheap.

Advantage is the value line of Priority Transportation, run from the same Memphis fleet, cleaned to the same standard, and supported by the same phone number.

Most budget rental companies save money in the places you notice — a dirty cabin, a counter queue, a bait rate that doubles at pickup. We'd rather save it in the places you don't. There's no airport counter to staff, no shuttle bus to run, and no commission-driven upsell script, because there's nobody standing between you and the car.

What you get instead is a clean, inspected, properly photographed vehicle delivered where you are, at the price shown on the site. Every car is the same one in the listing. Every rate includes the mileage and the fuel policy in plain language.

When a vehicle is out of service we leave it on the site and say so, because a fleet that claims everything is always available isn't telling the truth about anything.

Where we operate

Memphis and the surrounding metro, including delivery to Memphis International, downtown hotels, and addresses in Germantown, Collierville and Bartlett.

Looking for something more premium?
